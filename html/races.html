How?

I continue to race triathlons of various distances. The table below shows the standard distances for the common races. I prefer the Sprint and Olympic distances although I have completed the longer distances. The Long Course, Half, and Full distances are very challenging and require many hours of training.

Standard Triathlon Distances

Discipline Super Sprint Sprint Olympic Long Course Half Full
Swim 400 m 750 m 1.5 km 2 km 1.2 miles 2.4 miles
Bike 10 km 20 km 40 km 80 km 56 miles 112 miles
Run 2.5 km 5 km 10 km 20 km 13.1 miles 26.2 miles
Total 12.9 km 25.75 km 51.5 km 102 km 70.3 miles 140.6 miles

Triathlon distance information adapted from USA Triathlon
